## New sheets (read)

### U1. Verallia UK, Atomus 70cl spirits bottle (glass), read
- URL: https://uk.verallia.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Atomus.pdf
- Component: glass bottle. 1 page, A3 landscape, made in Canva (metadata), tagged PDF, text layer present.
- Weight: column header "WEIGHT" over the bare cell "500g" (header and value in separate text runs; the unit is attached to the number).
- Material: not stated at all (glass implied). Colour: "Available In: Standard Flint" (a trade colour term, not "clear"). Finish: "Available Finishes: Corkmouth". Capacity "700ml", height "207.4mm", diameter "89.0mm".
- Recyclability: none. Recycled content: only marketing prose, "industry leading weight and recycled content for a cork mouth bottle" (no number).
- Terms: "Product Designs are registered to Verallia UK Ltd." and "Please contact for further details." No copy licence.
- Messiness: weight stated but material missing, colour in trade vocabulary ("flint"), prose claim without a number, closure type only as a finish name. Dimensions drawing as an image.
- Exercises: 4 (colour wording "Standard Flint", recycled-content claim with no number, material absent), 5 (header/value split into separate runs), 2 in part (material slot missing, not weight).

## Not found in this round
- Encirc: no public data sheet found in search. Berry Global UK: no closure or container data sheet surfaced. Bericap UK: none (see above). UK corrugated or carton spec sheets with per-unit weight: none found (Smurfit Westrock and DS Smith publish board grades in gsm). Smurfit Westrock UK landing page https://www.smurfitkappa.com/uk/ppc/sustainable-packaging has no technical data.
- Pattern: UK glass and plastic bottle makers publish unit weight (g) on one-page sheets; closures, cartons and corrugated mostly do not publicly. That bias (public = bottles) should be stated in any report built on them.
