# T-135 QA: randomised differential test of the look-back

## Method
- Blind oracle: written from README, BRIEF, ingest/FORMAT.md, benchmark/examples/SOURCES.md, samples/generate.py and gov.uk guidance. No engine, report, loader, mapper or audit.py source and no existing tests were read. (One attempt to open an existing test file was blocked; nothing was read from it.)
- Where the docs are silent the oracle copied the CLI after black-box probes with hand-made inputs (listed as findings below).
- Generator: seeded `random.Random(seed)`, 1 to 3 workers per case; variable_hours and fixed_pay (25% of fixed with a blank contract cell, rate inferred); leave_year_start in {04-01, 01-01, 10-01, 02-15, 12-31, 06-01}; any weekday; histories of 1 to 51 weeks, 52 to 104, 105 to 140; gaps, statutory-only, bonus-only and overtime-only weeks; bonus_non_regular and expense_excluded noise; leave in runs of 1 to 7 weeks over one to several leave years; `weeks` fractions 1, 0.5, 0.25, 0.75, 0.1, 0.33 or blank/absent column; holiday_pay set to exact due, off by a penny or two, or far off; thresholds 0, 0.01, 0.50, 5.00.
- Comparison: worker, leave week, paid, due, difference, direction, `ref_weeks_used` from exceptions.csv, to the penny (oracle uses exact fractions, rounds half-up once at the end).
- Mutation check of the oracle: window 103 or 105 weeks, 51 or 53 reference weeks, 3 instead of 4 normal weeks each produce 140 to 290 failing cases of 330, so the test is sensitive to those boundaries.

## Result
- `tests/test_differential.py`: 330 seeded cases (about 4,500 exception rows, 30 s) plus a hand-computed sample check. All pass.
- Extra run of 1,500 further seeds (1000 to 2499, about 20,000 rows) outside the suite: 0 disagreements.
- Full suite: `Ran 293 tests in 30.047s` / `OK` (command in the task, from the product folder).
- No engine bug found, so no `expectedFailure` is used.

## Rules the oracle learned by probing (docs silent or ambiguous)
| # | Behaviour | Triage | Severity |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-1 | `cannot_check` rows carry a `due` and `difference`: variable_hours due 0.00 and difference = -paid; fixed_pay due = contract rate and difference = due - paid. Repro: fixed worker B, contract 100, holiday_pay 50 in its first week gives `50.00,100.00,50.00,cannot_check`. FORMAT.md says these are "not counted as an over- or underpayment", but a reader of the CSV sees an underpayment figure. The test ignores due/difference on these rows. | DOC AMBIGUITY. Recommend a line in FORMAT.md: "for cannot_check rows, due and difference are placeholders; ignore them", or blank them as the rolled-up flag rows do. | low |
| F-2 | Basic-only leave weeks (after the first 4 per leave year) use the average of basic over the most recent 52 weeks that have basic > 0, not the 52 paid weeks used for normal pay. Repro: 10 weeks basic 100 + overtime 10, then 20 overtime-only weeks of 40; leave week 5 of the year: due 100.00 (not 33.33). `ref_weeks_used` (30) does not describe that average. Defensible, but the docs say only "52 paid weeks". | DOC AMBIGUITY. Recommend: "the basic-only average uses the 52 most recent weeks with basic pay". | medium |
| F-3 | fixed_pay: weeks with no basic are not "paid weeks" even if they carry regular_overtime/commission, so that pay drops out of the variable average. Repro: contract 100; 4 weeks basic 100 + overtime 20; 1 week overtime-only 60; leave next week: due 120.00 (4 weeks used, 1 skipped), not 124.00. variable_hours counts overtime-only weeks as paid, so the two types differ. | ENGINE BUG (re-triaged by the Lead after reviewer CHANGES, reviews/T-135-review.md: gov.uk and BRIEF.md skip only weeks with no pay; engine/lookback.py fixed_pay skips weeks with zero basic, understating due). Fixed in T-135 on this branch. | medium |
| F-4 | A leave row that straddles the 4-week point (for example 3.5 weeks taken, then a 1.0 row) is split pro rata: 0.5 at normal pay, 0.5 at basic. Fractions accumulate across rows. Not stated in the docs. | DOC AMBIGUITY. Recommend a line in FORMAT.md. | low |
| F-5 | Reference window is exactly the 104 weeks before the leave week (week-104 included). Agrees with "up to 104 weeks". | none | n/a |
| F-6 | Average is kept unrounded and the due amount is rounded once to the penny (matches the E13 note in SOURCES.md). | none | n/a |
| F-7 | Exception = |difference| > threshold; a difference equal to the threshold is not listed. Matches "above `--threshold`". | none | n/a |

## Disagreements
None remaining. Every disagreement seen while building the oracle was an ORACLE BUG or a probe finding in the table above:
1. Rounding the average to the penny before multiplying (ORACLE BUG, fixed: unrounded average).
2. fixed_pay paid weeks (F-3) and cannot_check values (F-1), found as the only residual mismatches after the first fix.

## Not covered
Rolled-up workers, `contract_weekly_basic` changing mid-history (flags only; the rate inferred from a varying basic was not modelled), real-world payroll shapes, and report.md content.
