# T-136 QA (part A): blind oracle for a changing contract basic and for rolled-up holiday pay

## Method
Blind: no engine/, report/, loader or mapper source read. Sources: ingest/FORMAT.md, README.md, BRIEF.md, reviews/T-135-qa.md, tests/test_differential.py (helpers copied, not edited), tests/test_rolled_up.py (documented behaviour only), gov.uk rules, plus black-box CLI probes where the docs are silent. Test file: `tests/test_t136_oracle.py` (7 tests, about 13 s). Seeded `random.Random`.

## Cases run
- Part 1, fixed_pay with a moving basic: 55 seeds (1 to 2 workers each, about 80 workers). Two modes: contract cell blank (rate inferred, basic moves 1 to 3 times, ties, gaps, overtime-only recent weeks, no basic in the last 8 weeks) and contract supplied while the history basic moves (contract equal to old level, new level or unrelated). Rolled-up pay rows are mixed into these non-rolled-up workers (must never count). Compared per exception row: worker, week, paid, due, difference, direction, `ref_weeks_used`; second test compares `fixed_basic_rate` and the rate/flag columns.
- Part 2, irregular_rolled_up: 60 seeds, 1 to 3 rolled-up workers each, leave_year_start in {04-01, 01-01, 10-01, 06-01, or exactly a pay week's date}, weeks before and after 2024-04-01, rolled-up row exact / off by 1p / random / zero / absent, holiday_pay in some weeks, contract cell 250.00 / 0 / blank (ignored), leave.csv rows for rolled-up workers (must not be re-audited), and in half the cases a variable_hours worker beside them. Compared `rolled_up_pay` rows (paid, due, difference, direction, flag) and, in a second test, the stdout line `N rolled-up pay weeks checked, M exceptions.`.
- Three hand-computed pinning tests (F1, F2, F5 below).

## Result
No ENGINE BUG found. No expectedFailure used. Every disagreement during construction was an ORACLE BUG or an undocumented rule learned by probing (F2 to F4 below).
Rolled-up path: zero disagreements with the documented rules, including stdout counts, 12.07% half-up rounding, the 2024 boundary with non-default leave_year_start, and leave weeks of rolled-up workers not being re-audited.

## Findings
| id | behaviour | repro | triage | severity |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T136-F1 | `contract_weekly_basic` is one value per worker; it cannot change over time. Every leave week, before or after a pay rise, is audited at that one rate. The only signal is the flag `reference_week_basic_differs_from_contract`. A leave week taken before a rise, paid correctly at the old rate, shows as underpaid by the whole rise. gov.uk: use the rate in force at the time of leave. | contract 200.00, basic 100 for 10 weeks, leave week 11: due 200.00 vs the 100-based pay (HandComputed.test_f1) | DOC AMBIGUITY / design limitation (FORMAT.md says a single value but not what happens to earlier leave). Recommend a FORMAT.md sentence, or an effective-dated contract rate column. | medium |
| T136-F2 | Blank contract: the inferred rate is the MODE of basic over the 8 calendar weeks before the leave week, ties to the most recently seen value; if those 8 weeks hold no basic, the latest basic in the 104-week window (`latest_week_fallback`). Not in FORMAT.md or README (only "inferred and flagged"). Consequence: a rise 1 to 3 weeks before the leave week is not picked up (old rate used, flag `latest_week_basic_differs_from_rate_used`). | basic 100 x17 then 200 x3, leave next week: rate 100.00; 100 x16 then 200 x4: rate 200.00 | DOC AMBIGUITY | medium |
| T136-F3 | Blank contract and the same inference across a long gap of basic (fallback) reuses an old rate silently apart from the generic inferred flag. | basic 10 weeks, 10 weeks overtime only: rate 100.00 (fallback) | DOC AMBIGUITY (part of F2) | low |
| T136-F4 | `latest_week_basic_differs_from_rate_used` compares the basic of the calendar week immediately before the leave week (0 if none) with the rate. A gap week, or the previous week being itself a leave week, raises it though the rate never changed, so consecutive leave weeks of a steady worker are all flagged. Flag names are not listed in FORMAT.md. | steady basic 118.57, leave in a week preceded by an unpaid week: flag present | DOC AMBIGUITY (noisy flag) | low |
| T136-F5 | Blank contract with no basic anywhere in the history (overtime only): rate 0.00 and `latest_week_fallback`, no specific warning that the contractual basic is unknown. Due is the overtime average only, which understates a fixed-pay worker. | 6 weeks overtime 30, leave week 7: `fixed_basic_rate` 0.00, due 30.00 (HandComputed.test_f5) | DOC AMBIGUITY, suggest a dedicated flag | low |

Triage notes: F1 to F5 are pinned to the current behaviour in the tests so a change is noticed. If the owner decides F1 or F5 is a bug, flip the matching hand test to the gov.uk figure.
