# Standard mobilisation plan

**Document ref:** HF-MOB-16 | **Version:** 3.0 | **Date:** 10 August 2026

Our standard plan runs for 6 weeks before service start, led by the Operations Director.

| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| -6 | Contract kick-off; confirm specification, access, keys, and client contacts; start TUPE information request |
| -5 | Site surveys and risk assessments; draft method statements issued for client comment |
| -4 | Recruit or confirm staffing; order equipment and chemicals; DBS and licence checks |
| -3 | TUPE consultation meetings; uniforms and ID issued |
| -2 | Induction and site training; assets and keys logged; client sign-off of method statements |
| -1 | Dry-run shifts with supervisors; out-of-hours contact tested |
| 0 | Service start; Operations Director on site for first 3 days |
| +1 to +4 | Weekly review meeting with client; first monthly audit at week 4 |

## Evidence
- **Eastvale Borough Council (fictional) offices and libraries:** mobilised in 5 weeks with 17 TUPE transfers; first-month audit score 88%, reaching 92% by month 3.
- **Tarn Valley Council (fictional) parks:** mobilised in 4 weeks, first mowing round completed on day 3.
- **Marlowe Fen District Council (fictional) leisure centre:** mobilised in 3 weeks with 6 new security officers licensed before start.
- Shortest mobilisation delivered: 3 weeks. We have not mobilised a contract in under 3 weeks.
