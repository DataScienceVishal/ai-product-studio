# Case study: Eastvale Borough Council (fictional), offices and libraries cleaning

**Document ref:** HF-CS-20 | **Date:** 12 October 2026

- **Client:** Eastvale Borough Council (fictional), a district council.
- **Service:** daily cleaning of 9 buildings: the Civic Offices (4 floors), 5 libraries, 2 community halls. About 14,200 square metres.
- **Contract:** £412,000 a year; 3 years from 1 April 2022 plus a 2-year extension, now running to 31 March 2027. Our largest contract.
- **Team:** 22 operatives and 3 supervisors (17 TUPE transferees plus 8 new hires).

## Challenge
The previous contractor left with an audit score of 71% and 6 complaints a month. The council wanted fewer complaints and a visible improvement within 3 months.

## What we did
Mobilised in 5 weeks (HF-MOB-16); introduced colour coding and a weekly 20-point inspection; moved library cleaning to before opening; switched to concentrated eco-label chemicals and HEPA vacuums.

## Results
- Audit score: 88% in month 1, 92% by month 3, 93.1% average in 2025/26.
- Complaints fell from about 6 a month under the previous contractor to an average of 1.3 a month.
- Recycling of office waste rose from 44% to 61% by weight.
- Staff turnover on the contract: 16% in 2025/26.
- The council's contract manager's feedback is held on file; no quotation is reproduced in this library.
