# Case study: Marlowe Fen District Council (fictional), leisure centre security

**Document ref:** HF-CS-21 | **Date:** 12 October 2026

- **Client:** Marlowe Fen District Council (fictional).
- **Service:** static guarding at Marlowe Fen Leisure Centre (fictional), a pool and sports hall with about 410,000 visits a year; reception and car park patrols, evening lock-up.
- **Contract:** £168,000 a year; started 3 October 2022; 3 years plus 2 optional; extended in October 2025 to 2 October 2027.
- **Team:** 6 dedicated licensed officers plus relief cover; 2 officers on site at peak evenings.

## Challenge
Anti-social behaviour in the car park and evening disorder had led to 31 recorded incidents in the six months before we started.

## What we did
Three-week mobilisation (6 new officers licensed before start); conflict management training for all officers; a joint incident review with the centre manager each month; improved lighting reported to the council.

## Results
- Recorded incidents fell from 31 in the 6 months before our start to 12 in the first 6 months and 9 in the 6 months to 31 March 2026.
- 100% of shifts staffed in 2025/26.
- Zero formal complaints about officer conduct in 2025/26.
- Average response to the centre manager's calls: under 5 minutes on site.
