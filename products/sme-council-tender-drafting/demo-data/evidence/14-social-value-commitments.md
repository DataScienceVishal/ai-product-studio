# Social value commitments and delivery record

**Document ref:** HF-SV-14 | **Version:** 2.3 | **Date:** 5 November 2026

## Delivered in 2025/26 (all contracts)
- **Local jobs:** 28 of 33 new hires (85%) lived within 10 miles of the contract site. 6 hires came from the long-term unemployed through a local employment partner (fictional: Fenborough Works Partnership).
- **Apprenticeships:** 6 apprenticeship starts since 2022 (2 in 2025/26); 4 completed, 2 in progress.
- **Pay:** hourly floor for operatives £13.10 from 1 April 2026. **We are not a Living Wage Foundation accredited employer** and have no application in progress.
- **Hours:** 88% of staff are on contracted hours of 16 or more a week; 12 staff (12%) are on zero-hours contracts.
- **Volunteering:** 82 staff days in 2025/26 (about 0.8 days per head), including litter picks and a food bank.
- **Local spend:** 63% of supplier spend (by value) went to businesses within 40 miles of Fenborough.
- **Community grounds work:** 5 community planting days with Tarn Valley Council (fictional) parks, 118 volunteers in total.

## Commitments for a new contract (offered, not yet delivered)
- At least one apprenticeship or work-experience place per £250,000 of annual contract value.
- 10 staff volunteering days a year per contract.
- Quarterly reporting against a measures framework agreed with the council.

## Not available
