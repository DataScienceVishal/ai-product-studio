# Data protection and information security policy

**Document ref:** HF-DP-12 | **Version:** 3.1 | **Approved:** 20 July 2026

- We process personal data of our employees and, on some sites, hold key-holder lists and incident reports for clients as a data processor under UK GDPR.
- Registered with the data protection regulator (demo reference: ICO-DEMO-0001).
- **Controls:** laptops encrypted; multi-factor sign-in on email and HR systems; access to client key lists limited to 4 named roles; paper incident books locked in site safes.
- Cyber Essentials (basic) certified, CERT-DEMO-0002, valid to 3 September 2027. **We do not hold ISO 27001 or Cyber Essentials Plus.**
- **Breach history:** 1 personal-data incident in the last 5 years (a misdirected email with a rota, 11 staff, reported to the client within 24 hours, no regulator action). 0 incidents in 2025/26.
- Retention: CCTV and incident records are held for 31 days unless needed for a case; HR records for 6 years after leaving.
- Data protection training is annual (95% complete, HF-TRN-08).
- No staff or client data is held outside the UK.
