# Health and safety policy

**Document ref:** HF-HS-06 | **Version:** 6.1 | **Approved:** 12 January 2026 by the Managing Director | **Review:** annually

## Statement
We will provide a safe place and system of work for employees, clients and the public, in line with the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974. We are not certified to ISO 45001; our system is assessed through CERT-DEMO-0004 (contractor pre-qualification, valid to 19 January 2027).

## Arrangements
- **Competent adviser:** the Quality, Health and Safety Manager holds a NEBOSH-style general certificate (demo qualification).
- **Risk assessments and method statements:** written for every site before mobilisation and reviewed every 12 months or after any incident.
- **Training:** manual handling, COSHH, working at height (grounds), lone working (security) and fire marshal; matrix in HF-TRN-08.
- **Incidents:** all accidents are logged. In 2025/26 we recorded 9 minor accidents, 1 RIDDOR-reportable injury (a slip, 8 days off work) and 0 enforcement notices.
- **Lost-time injury rate:** 1.7 per 100 employees in 2025/26.
- **Lone working:** security officers on single-manned sites check in by phone every 60 minutes.
- **Contractors:** any subcontractor must show insurance and a risk assessment before starting.
- **Consultation:** a monthly safety briefing for each contract; toolbox talks for grounds crews every Monday.
