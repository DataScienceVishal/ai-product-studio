# Business continuity plan (summary)

**Document ref:** HF-BCP-13 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Last tested:** 18 September 2026 (desktop exercise)

- **Scenarios covered:** severe weather, loss of the Kestrel Works depot, mass staff absence (for example 25% absent), supplier failure, loss of IT, fuel shortage.
- **Staff cover:** a bank of 9 pre-vetted relief cleaners and 4 relief security officers, available at 4 hours' notice. In winter 2025/26 this covered 100% of shifts during a 3-day snow period at Tarn Valley Council (fictional).
- **Recovery targets:** critical services (security posts, school cleaning) restored within 4 hours; routine cleaning and grounds within 24 hours.
- **Stock:** 4 weeks of consumables held at the depot; the depot holds a duplicate set of site keys in a secure store.
- **Call-out:** a 24-hour duty manager mobile number is given to each client.
- **Pandemic:** we kept 100% of security posts staffed and 96% of cleaning shifts delivered during 2020 to 2021 (internal record).
- **Limits:** we have no second depot and no ISO 22301 certification. Plan testing is once a year.

Fictional document for a software demo. Not a real company.
