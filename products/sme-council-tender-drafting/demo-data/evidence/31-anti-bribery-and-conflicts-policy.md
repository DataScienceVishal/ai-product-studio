# Anti-bribery, gifts and conflicts of interest policy

**Document ref:** HF-AB-31 | **Version:** 1.5 | **Approved:** 8 April 2026

- Zero tolerance of bribery and corruption (Bribery Act 2010).
- Gifts and hospitality above £25 must be declared in a register held by the Finance and HR Manager; no gifts are offered to council officers or members.
- Conflicts of interest are declared by the Managing Director, directors and managers at appointment and every January; the register for 2026 shows 0 conflicts relevant to any council client.
- No director or shareholder is a councillor or employed by a client council.
- **Exclusion grounds:** no director has been convicted of any offence listed under the mandatory exclusion grounds; no company in the group has been subject to insolvency proceedings; no grave professional misconduct finding; no contract terminated for default. (Statement of the Managing Director, 3 November 2026.)
- Tax: all taxes and National Insurance are up to date (accountant's letter dated 28 September 2026).
- Training: all managers briefed in April 2026.
