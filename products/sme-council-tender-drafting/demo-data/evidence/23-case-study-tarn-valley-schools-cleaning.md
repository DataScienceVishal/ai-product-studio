# Case study: Tarn Valley Council (fictional), primary schools cleaning

**Document ref:** HF-CS-23 | **Date:** 12 October 2026

- **Client:** Tarn Valley Council (fictional), for 8 maintained primary schools (about 1,900 pupils).
- **Contract:** £97,000 a year; started 1 September 2024 for 3 years; ends 31 August 2027.
- **Team:** 11 operatives and 1 supervisor, all enhanced-DBS checked (HF-SG-11).

## What we did
Cleaning before 8:00 and after 16:30; summer holiday deep clean of all 8 schools in the first 3 weeks of August each year; touch-point cleaning twice a day; safeguarding awareness for all staff.

## Results
- Average inspection score 90.2% in 2025/26.
- Deep clean of all 8 schools completed 2 days ahead of the agreed date in August 2025.
- 3 complaints in 2025/26, all resolved within 5 working days.
- 0 safeguarding concerns recorded.
- Headteachers' satisfaction survey (autumn 2025): 7 of 8 schools rated the service "good" or "excellent" (survey held on file; not a published statistic).
