# Supply chain, subcontracting and chemicals policy

**Document ref:** HF-SUP-27 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 14 May 2026

- **Suppliers:** 23 active suppliers; the three largest are Demo Janitorial Supplies Ltd, Demo Machinery Hire Ltd and Demo Uniforms Ltd (all fictional), together 58% of supplier spend.
- **Subcontracting:** limited to alarm receiving (Demo ARC Ltd), tree work above 3 metres (Demo Arborists Ltd), and high-level window cleaning above 3 metres (Demo Access Ltd). We would tell the client before any subcontracting begins and remain responsible for the work.
- **Payment:** subcontractors are paid within 30 days; average payment time was 27 days in 2025/26.
- **Chemicals:** safety data sheets held in a site folder for every product; 92% of cleaning chemicals by volume are independently eco-labelled; no products classified as very toxic to aquatic life are used.
- **Plastics:** bin liners in 100% recycled content since 2025; no single-use plastic sprays.
- **Suppliers' ethics:** all suppliers are sent our modern slavery questionnaire (HF-MS-09).
- **Not in place:** a supplier carbon-reporting requirement, supplier audits, or a local-supplier target in writing.
