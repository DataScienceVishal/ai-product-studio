# TUPE approach

**Document ref:** HF-TUPE-15 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Date:** 2 June 2026

## Principles
We honour the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of Employment) Regulations 2006. Transferring staff keep their terms, continuous service and pension rights as the law requires. We do not plan to reduce headcount or pay as a reason for taking on a transfer.

## Process (8 weeks before service start where the timetable allows)
1. Request employee liability information (ELI) from the client or outgoing contractor at least 28 days before transfer.
2. Meet the outgoing contractor and client within 5 working days of ELI.
3. Hold a group briefing and 1-to-1 meetings for every transferring employee, by the Operations Director and Finance and HR Manager.
4. Confirm pensions, holiday and sickness records, and any disciplinary matters.
5. Issue a welcome pack and new contract on day one.
6. Check pay is correct in the first payroll run.

## Experience
- **Eastvale Borough Council (fictional), 1 April 2022:** 17 cleaning staff transferred in; 17 of 17 remained after 6 months; 12 remain in October 2026.
- **Tarn Valley Council (fictional), 1 June 2023:** 5 grounds staff transferred in; 4 remain.
- Our largest TUPE transfer is **17 staff**. We have not managed a transfer over 20 staff, and we have not had a transfer involving local government pension scheme admitted-body status.
