# Safeguarding policy

**Document ref:** HF-SG-11 | **Version:** 2.2 | **Approved:** 15 May 2026

- Applies to staff working in schools, libraries, leisure centres and parks where children or adults at risk may be present.
- **Designated safeguarding lead:** the Contracts Manager (cleaning), deputy: the Operations Director. Both completed level 3 safeguarding training in March 2026.
- All staff on school and library contracts hold an enhanced DBS check with a barred-list check where eligible; checks are renewed every 3 years. 32 staff are currently covered; 30 of 32 have in-date level 2 awareness training (see HF-TRN-08).
- Staff are never left alone with a child; cleaning in schools is carried out before 8:00 or after 16:30 term-time, with site-staff presence where required.
- Concerns are reported to the designated lead within the same shift, and to the client's lead the same day. We recorded 0 safeguarding concerns in 2025/26.
- Lost or damaged keys and fobs for school sites are reported to the client within 2 hours.
