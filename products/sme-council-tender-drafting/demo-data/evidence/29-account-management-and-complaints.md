# Account management, reporting and complaints procedure

**Document ref:** HF-AM-29 | **Version:** 3.0 | **Date:** 25 June 2026

## Account management
- Each contract has a named Contracts Manager (a role) and a supervisor on site. The Operations Director attends quarterly review meetings.
- **Meetings:** weekly for the first 4 weeks, then monthly service review, and a quarterly strategic review.
- **Reporting:** a monthly performance report (audit scores, complaints, attendance, incidents, stock) sent within 5 working days of month end.
- **Contact hours:** office 07:00 to 18:00 Monday to Friday; 24-hour duty manager.

## Complaints procedure
1. Log the complaint on the day received.
2. Acknowledge within 1 working day.
3. Investigate and respond within 5 working days (10 for complex issues).
4. Record cause and corrective action; the Quality, Health and Safety Manager reviews every complaint monthly.
5. If unresolved, escalate to the Managing Director within 5 further working days.

## Service credits
We have agreed service credit schemes on 2 contracts. We paid **£1,140** in service credits in 2025/26 (all on the Eastvale contract in the first quarter of the year). We have never had a contract notice of default.
