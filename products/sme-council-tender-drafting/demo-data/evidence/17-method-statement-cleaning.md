# Method statement: commercial cleaning

**Document ref:** HF-MS-CLN-17 | **Version:** 4.2 | **Date:** 1 September 2026

## Scope
Daily cleaning of offices, libraries, schools and communal areas; periodic deep cleans; washroom and kitchenette servicing; window cleaning to a height of 3 metres from the ground using water-fed poles.

## Method
- **Colour-coded system:** red (washrooms floors), blue (general), green (kitchens), yellow (sanitary fittings); microfibre cloths laundered at 60 degrees.
- **Daily:** vacuum, damp-dust, empty bins, clean washrooms, refill consumables, wipe touch points twice daily in public buildings.
- **Periodic:** carpet extraction every 6 months; hard floor machine scrub and seal quarterly; high-level dusting every 3 months; windows every 8 weeks.
- **Supervision:** one working supervisor per 7 operatives; weekly inspection scored on a 20-point sheet; monthly joint inspection with the client's contract manager.
- **Equipment:** battery-powered vacuums with HEPA filters (all 22 machines); no aerosol sprays.
- **Consumables:** a fortnightly stock check; consumable usage reported monthly.
- **Out of hours:** fault reporting through a duty manager line 24 hours a day.
- **Infection control:** enhanced regimes (touch-point cleaning, colour coding) for general buildings only. **We do not provide clinical or healthcare cleaning** (no NHS National Cleaning Standards experience).
