# Quality policy (ISO 9001-style)

**Document ref:** HF-QMS-04 | **Version:** 5.0 | **Approved:** 6 January 2026 by the Managing Director | **Review:** annually

Harrowmere Facilities Ltd (fictional) operates a quality management system certified to an ISO 9001-style standard (CERT-DEMO-0001, valid to 11 June 2029).

## Commitments
1. Meet the written specification and service levels of every contract, measured monthly.
2. Inspect every site: supervisors inspect weekly, the Contracts Manager audits monthly, and the Quality, Health and Safety Manager audits each contract quarterly. Our 2025/26 average audit score across the three council contracts was **91.4%** against a target of 90%.
3. Log every complaint within 1 working day, acknowledge within 1 working day and resolve within 5 working days. In 2025/26 we received 37 complaints and resolved 94% (35) inside 5 working days.
4. Run a documented corrective-action process: root cause recorded, action owner named, closed within 20 working days.
5. Review the system at a management review each January and set measurable objectives.

## Objectives for 2026/27
- Audit score of 92% or higher.
- Complaints under 30 for the year.
- 100% of operatives complete induction before their first shift unsupervised.
