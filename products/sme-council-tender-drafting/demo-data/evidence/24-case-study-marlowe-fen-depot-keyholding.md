# Case study: Marlowe Fen District Council (fictional), depot security and key-holding

**Document ref:** HF-CS-24 | **Date:** 12 October 2026

- **Client:** Marlowe Fen District Council (fictional).
- **Service:** overnight mobile patrols and key-holding for the waste and highways depot and 6 other council buildings; alarm response through Demo ARC Ltd (fictional).
- **Contract:** £58,000 a year; started 1 April 2024; 2 years plus 2 optional; runs to 31 March 2028.
- **Team:** 4 licensed officers on rotation.

## Results
- 64 alarm call-outs in 2025/26; average attendance 22 minutes against a 30-minute target.
- 0 break-ins at the depot since start (the council recorded 3 in the year before).
- Fuel theft: none reported since 1 April 2024.
- Contract extension to 2028 agreed in March 2026 (the council issued a letter, held on file).

Note: this contract is for security services only. The depot's waste services are run by the council; **we have no waste collection experience.**
