# Whistleblowing policy

**Document ref:** HF-WB-30 | **Version:** 2.0 | **Approved:** 8 April 2026

- Any employee or subcontractor may raise a concern about wrongdoing, safety, modern slavery or poor service, in confidence, without fear of detriment (Public Interest Disclosure Act 1998).
- **Routes:** the Finance and HR Manager; the Managing Director; or an independent helpline run by Demo Advice Line Ltd (fictional), open 24 hours.
- Concerns are acknowledged in 2 working days and a decision on investigation is taken within 10 working days.
- The policy is part of induction (100% of staff in HF-TRN-08).
- **Record:** 2 concerns raised in 2025/26 (one about a rota error, one about uniform supply); both closed. No concern related to safeguarding or modern slavery.
