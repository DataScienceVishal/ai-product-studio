# Case study: Tarn Valley Council (fictional), parks and open spaces grounds maintenance

**Document ref:** HF-CS-22 | **Date:** 12 October 2026

- **Client:** Tarn Valley Council (fictional), a unitary council.
- **Service:** grounds maintenance of 12 parks and 4 cemeteries' grass areas, about 86 hectares.
- **Contract:** £146,000 a year; 1 June 2023 for 3 years plus 2 optional; extended to 31 May 2028.
- **Team:** 6 operatives (5 TUPE transferees, 1 apprentice).

## Challenge
The council wanted to cut herbicide use, increase biodiversity and keep grass-cutting frequencies without extra cost.

## What we did
Re-planned cutting rounds (saving about 11% of mileage); introduced no-mow zones and wildflower verges; moved to spot treatment of weeds only; ran 5 community planting days.

## Results
- Herbicide use down 64% by volume between 2022 and 2025.
- Wildflower verges extended by 3,100 square metres.
- 100% of scheduled cuts completed in 2025/26 (24 formal and 18 amenity cuts per site).
- 118 community volunteers took part in planting days.
- Winter gritting: 24 call-outs in 2025/26, all completed before 7:00.
- 2 complaints about missed cuts in 2025/26, resolved within 3 working days.
