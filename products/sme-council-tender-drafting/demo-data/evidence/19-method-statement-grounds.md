# Method statement: grounds maintenance

**Document ref:** HF-MS-GRD-19 | **Version:** 3.0 | **Date:** 1 September 2026

## Scope
Grass cutting, hedge and shrub maintenance, litter picking, leaf clearance, bed maintenance, sports pitch marking, weed control and winter gritting of car parks (not highways).

## Method
- **Programme:** an annual schedule agreed with the client in March. Amenity grass: 18 cuts a year; formal grass 24 cuts; hedges twice a year outside bird-nesting season (not between 1 March and 31 August unless checked).
- **Crews:** two 3-person crews, each with a working crew leader, plus 2 operatives on smaller contracts; 8 operatives in total.
- **Machinery:** 4 ride-on mowers, 6 pedestrian mowers, hand tools; all machinery serviced every 6 months.
- **Environment:** peat-free compost; wildflower verges; herbicide used only for spot treatment by certified staff (3 of 8 hold the certificate; HF-TRN-08).
- **Tree work:** we do not carry out tree surgery or climbing; trees above 3 metres are subcontracted to Demo Arborists Ltd (fictional).
- **Winter:** car park gritting with a trailer spreader; 24 call-outs in winter 2025/26, all completed before 7:00.
- **Reporting:** a fortnightly schedule-versus-actual report to the client.
- **Not provided:** play-area inspection (ROSPA-style), sports turf renovation, large-scale landscaping capital works.
