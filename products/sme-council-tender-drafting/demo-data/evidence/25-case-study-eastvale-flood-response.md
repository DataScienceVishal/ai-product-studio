# Case study: Eastvale Borough Council (fictional), emergency flood clean-up

**Document ref:** HF-CS-25 | **Date:** 12 October 2026

- **Client:** Eastvale Borough Council (fictional); ad hoc order under the cleaning contract (HF-CS-20).
- **Event:** burst water main flooded the ground floor of Eastvale Central Library (fictional) at 22:40 on 14 November 2024.
- **Value:** £46,000 (a variation outside the £412,000 annual value).

## Response
- Duty manager called out at 23:10; 6 operatives on site by 01:30.
- Wet extraction and drying equipment hired in within 5 hours; water removed from about 900 square metres by 20:00 the same day.
- Specialist dehumidifiers run for 6 days; carpets removed and disposed of as general waste.
- The library reopened to the public on Monday 25 November 2024, 11 days after the flood, with no staff injuries and no additional complaints.

## Limits
We are not a restoration or fire-and-flood specialist; this was a clean-up and drying support job. We held no specialist restoration accreditation.
