# Equality, diversity and inclusion policy

**Document ref:** HF-EDI-10 | **Version:** 3.0 | **Approved:** 8 April 2026

- We do not discriminate on any protected characteristic under the Equality Act 2010 in recruitment, pay, promotion or dismissal.
- Vacancies are advertised openly and interviews use a standard scoring sheet. Reasonable adjustments are offered at interview and in work.
- Workforce profile at 31 October 2026 (self-declared, 102 staff, 97 responses): 47% women, 29% aged 50 or over, 18% from ethnic minority backgrounds, 9% declare a disability.
- Pay: our median hourly rate gap between women and men was 2.1% in 2025/26 (below 250 employees so no statutory reporting; calculated voluntarily).
- Language: induction material is available in English; we have translated the induction handbook into Polish and Urdu. We have **no Welsh-language policy** and no Welsh-speaking staff recorded.
- Complaints under this policy: 1 in 2025/26, investigated and closed in 12 working days.
- The Finance and HR Manager owns this policy. Managers complete equality awareness training every 3 years; 6 of 7 are in date.
