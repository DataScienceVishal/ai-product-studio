# Section 1: Instructions

## 1.1 About the Trust

Wrenbourne Academy Trust (fictional) is a multi-academy trust running four primary schools and one secondary school with about 2,900 pupils in total. The Trust wants one cleaning provider that understands school environments, works safely around children and delivers consistent standards.

## 1.2 What to submit

1. A completed Section 3 (Compliance and Suitability).
2. Answers to Section 5 (Quality Submission), using the question numbers and the limits shown.
3. A completed Pricing Workbook (Section 6).
4. Supporting certificates and policies.

## 1.3 Submission method

All tenders must be uploaded to the Trust's procurement portal. Submissions made after 12 noon on the closing day will not be opened.

## 1.4 Format

Responses must be typed in 11 point font with single line spacing. For questions limited by pages, a side means one side of A4 with margins of at least 2 cm. Word limits are strict and evaluators will stop reading at the limit.

## 1.5 Clarifications

Please use the portal message system. The Trust will share all answers with all bidders.

## 1.6 Safeguarding on visits

Anyone visiting a school site must sign in, wear a visitor badge and be escorted. Visitors must carry photographic identification. The Trust may ask for evidence of DBS status for visitors, and visits to areas with pupils will only take place with a member of Trust staff.

## 1.7 Data protection

Any personal data shared by the Trust during the procurement (for example in the TUPE information) is shared for the purpose of preparing a tender only and must be handled under UK GDPR, kept securely and deleted if the bidder is unsuccessful.

## 1.8 Reservation of rights

The Trust is not obliged to award a contract and is not responsible for any costs incurred in tendering.

# Section 2: Specification

## 2.1 The schools

|---|---|---|---|---|

## 2.2 Hours

Standard cleaning is carried out between 15:30 and 20:00 on school days. A small day team is needed on every site between 10:30 and 14:00 for toilet checks and lunchtime spills. Holiday deep cleans take place in the weeks after the end of term, with a summer holiday programme of at least 4 weeks. Community lets in the evenings (St Oswin's, Wrenbourne High sports hall) may require reactive cleans after 21:00.

## 2.3 Day-to-day tasks

- Classrooms: empty bins, vacuum or sweep, damp mop, wipe tables, clean boards and sinks.
- Toilets: clean, disinfect and restock. A second pass takes place by the day team.
- Corridors, halls and dining rooms: sweep and mop, clean tables and seating after lunch.
- Specialist spaces: science laboratories (surface cleaning only, no handling of chemicals or specimens), food technology rooms, art rooms, design workshops.
- SEN unit and nursery: use of designated colour-coded equipment and child-safe chemicals. Frequent touch-point disinfection.
- External: litter-pick entrances and playground edges weekly.

## 2.4 Periodic tasks

| Task | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Machine scrub of hard floors | Termly, plus deep clean in summer |
| Carpet extraction | Annually in summer |
| Internal window cleaning | Twice per year |
| Radiators, high dusting, ceiling vents | Termly |
| Kitchen deep clean and extraction canopy exterior | In every school holiday, with the Trust's kitchen contractor |
| Furniture deep clean (desks, chairs, soft furnishings) | Annually in summer |

## 2.5 Standards and compliance

- Staff training to BICSc standards or equivalent.
- Cleaning in line with Department for Education guidance on cleaning of school premises and the Health and Safety Executive's COSHH requirements.
- All chemicals must be approved by the Trust's Estates Team. Products with strong fragrance or known respiratory irritants will not be accepted in classrooms.
- The Contractor must supply and hold current COSHH risk assessments and safety data sheets at each site.
- Allergen awareness: staff must not eat or store food in classrooms, and they must follow nut-free area rules.

## 2.6 Safeguarding

All staff working on the contract must hold an enhanced DBS check with barred list check, or a position on a DBS update service, before starting work. Staff must wear photographic ID, report concerns to the school's Designated Safeguarding Lead at once, must not be alone with a pupil, and must complete safeguarding training (Keeping Children Safe in Education, Part 1 as a minimum) every year. The Contractor must keep a single central record of all staff checks and make it available for inspection within 24 hours.

## 2.7 Site manager relationship and supervision

Each school will have a nominated cleaning supervisor, who will meet the school's site manager weekly. A contract manager will meet the Trust Director of Estates every month. The Contractor must hold keys securely and report losses in 2 hours.

## 2.8 Consumables

The Contractor supplies cleaning chemicals and equipment. Toilet paper, hand towels and soap are provided by the Trust under its own supply contract, and the Contractor will restock dispensers.

## 2.9 Waste and environment

Waste is segregated into general, recycling and food waste at each school. The Contractor must reduce plastic use, use refillable systems and report annually on chemical consumption. The Trust has set a net zero target for 2040.

## 2.10 TUPE

TUPE applies. The Trust expects that 41 staff will transfer, with a combined 880 hours per week. Details are in the TUPE schedule for bidders who pass the compliance stage. The Trust intends that the successful bidder pays the Real Living Wage at a minimum.

## 2.11 Performance measures

| Measure | Target |
|---|---|
| Termly joint inspection score | At least 85 percent per school |
| Safeguarding checks on central record | 100 percent complete |
| Cleaning complaints acknowledged | Within 1 working day |
| Holiday deep clean completed on time | 100 percent |
| Staff turnover | Under 25 percent per year |

# Section 4: Evaluation

## 4.1 Method

Only tenders that pass Section 3 will be scored. The Trust will use a panel made up of the Director of Estates, a headteacher representative and a finance officer.

## 4.2 Weightings

| Element | Weight |
|---|---|
| Quality, including social value | 60% |
| Price | 40% |

Social value (10%) is included within the quality total of 60%.

## 4.3 Scoring scale

Quality questions are scored from 0 to 5 using the following definitions. Marks are multiplied by the weighting divided by 5.

- 0: no response or fails to answer
- 1: serious reservations
- 2: some reservations
- 3: satisfactory
- 4: good
- 5: outstanding

## 4.4 Price

The lowest price scores 40. Other prices score 40 multiplied by (lowest price divided by the price). The assessed price is the total of the annual price for three years.

# Section 5: Quality Submission

Weightings are percentages of the final total score. The quality total available is 60%.

## 5.1 Service delivery (each response: maximum two sides of A4)

### Question 2.1: Method statement (10%)

Set out your method for delivering term-time daily cleaning and holiday deep cleans in all five schools, with a worked example of a typical weekday at the secondary school and a typical day at a primary school with a nursery.

### Question 2.2: Mobilisation (8%)

Describe how you will mobilise the contract across five sites during the summer holiday window (28 June to 31 August 2027) so that all schools are ready to open on 1 September 2027. Include staffing, TUPE consultation, training, equipment and a risk log.

## 5.2 Safeguarding and conduct (Word limit: 300 per question)

### Question 3.1: Safeguarding and vetting (8%)

Explain how you will ensure all staff are safely recruited and vetted, how you will keep the central record up to date, how staff will be trained, and how you will respond if a safeguarding concern arises.

### Question 3.2: Conduct, lone working and key holding (4%)

Describe your rules for staff conduct on school premises, including interaction with pupils, mobile phones, working alone after hours, key holding and alarm responses.

## 5.3 Quality and safety

### Question 4.1: Quality monitoring (8%)

Describe how you will monitor and report quality, involve site managers, and deal with failures. Word limit: 400.

### Question 4.2: Health, safety and chemical management (6%)

Describe how you will manage health and safety, COSHH, safety data sheets, chemical storage in schools and the use of low-odour, child-safe products. Word limit: 400.

### Question 4.3: Case studies (6%)

Provide two case studies from schools or similar settings (for example children's centres, nurseries or colleges) completed within the last 4 years. Include how the transition was managed, how quality was maintained and what improvements were delivered. Maximum one side of A4 per case study.

# Section 6: Pricing

Please complete the workbook sheet by sheet. Each school has its own sheet.

Prices are fixed for the first year. In later years, the labour element will move in line with the Real Living Wage announced each autumn and other elements by CPI.

## 6.4 Payment

Monthly in arrears, 30 days from receipt of valid invoices. The Trust may withhold money for unremedied failures as set out in the contract.
