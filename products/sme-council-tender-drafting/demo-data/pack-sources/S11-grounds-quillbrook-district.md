# Section 1: Instructions

## 1.1 Overview

Quillbrook District Council (fictional) is seeking a contractor to maintain its sports pitches, parks, play areas and associated open spaces in the northern half of the district. The work includes cricket squares and outfields, football and rugby pitches, bowling greens, parks and the winter gritting of leisure centre car parks. The Council is working to reduce pesticide use and carbon, and wants to hear how bidders will help.

## 1.2 Submission format

1. The quality response must not exceed 20 pages of A4 in total, at 11 point type, including diagrams and tables. Appendices containing certificates, policies, CVs and a programme chart are excluded from the page count.
2. The page limit applies to the whole response, not to each question. You may divide the 20 pages as you wish, although you are advised that each question carries a different weighting.
3. Some questions also set a word limit. Where one is shown, it must be met within the page allocation.
4. The price response must be submitted as the Excel workbook supplied.

## 1.3 Clarification and addenda

Questions are to be sent through the portal. Answers will be shared with all bidders. A briefing visit is mandatory for any bidder wishing to propose use of the pitches' storage compound.

## 1.6 Selection Questionnaire (pass/fail)

The Selection Questionnaire follows the standard PPN 03/24 structure and is assessed as pass or fail. It has the following parts.

1. Supplier information: legal name, company number, registered office, size, and any consortium or subcontractor details.
2. Exclusion grounds: mandatory grounds such as fraud, corruption, labour market offences and modern slavery, and discretionary grounds such as breach of environmental obligations, professional misconduct and distressed finances. Bidders must declare any relevant event and any self-cleaning evidence.
3. Economic and financial standing: the last two years of accounts, the turnover threshold in P3 and a statement on credit rating or insolvency risk.
4. Technical ability: three references from contracts in the last five years, at least one for a public sector client with sports turf and one with a play area inspection service.
5. Health and safety: your policy, accident rate (RIDDOR), any enforcement action in the last five years and a copy of your SSIP certificate.
6. Insurance: certificates for the cover in P1 and P2, and professional indemnity of £2 million for play inspection advice.
7. Equality, environment and information security: confirmation of policies and Cyber Essentials, or equivalent controls.

Where a bidder relies on a subcontractor to meet a pass/fail requirement, the subcontractor must complete its own Selection Questionnaire Part 2.

## 1.7 Contract terms and conditions

The contract will be the Council's standard services contract. Key terms include monthly payment in arrears, termination for convenience on 6 months' notice after the second year, service credits for missed KPIs of up to 5% of the monthly invoice, and liability caps of £10 million for each public liability event. The contractor must provide a performance bond or parent company guarantee of £50,000 if it is a new company with less than three years of accounts.

# Section 2: Specification

## 2.1 Scope

The contractor will provide the following services across the sites in Schedule A:

- cutting and rolling of cricket squares and outfields
- marking, drainage maintenance, aeration and renovation of football and rugby pitches
- maintenance of two crown green bowling greens
- cutting of park grass and trimming of edges
- hedge, shrub and tree-base maintenance
- litter picking and bin emptying
- weekly play area inspections and minor repairs
- winter gritting of leisure centre car parks and key paths.

## 2.2 Schedule A: Site schedule

| Site | Type | Area | Cut frequency per year | Specific tasks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lowmere Sports Hub, senior football pitches (4) | Football | 3.6 ha | 28 in season (March to October) | Weekly marking in season, twice-yearly overseeding and verti-draining, goalmouth repairs |
| Lowmere Sports Hub, rugby pitch | Rugby | 1.4 ha | 22 | Marking every fortnight, spring and autumn renovations |
| Lowmere Cricket Ground | Cricket square and outfield | 2.2 ha | Square 45 (April to September), outfield 30 | Rolling, scarifying, top dressing, wicket preparation for 14 fixtures |
| Greenacre and Tilden Bowling Greens (2) | Bowls | 0.5 ha | 90 (daily in season) | Cutting to 5 mm, brushing, verti-cutting, irrigation checks |
| Foxcote Park | Park | 8.5 ha | 18 amenity, 3 meadow | Shrub beds 0.4 ha, hedges 1.2 km, wildflower margins |
| Meadowgate Recreation Ground | Park and play | 4.1 ha | 16 | Play area inspection, litter pick, 600 m path edges |
| Brookside Play Parks (3) | Play areas | 1.2 ha | 16 | Weekly inspections, quarterly operational inspections |
| Leisure centre car parks (2) | Winter gritting | 1.9 ha | Not applicable | Pre-treat on frost forecast, 1 November to 31 March, up to 30 call-outs |

## 2.3 Standards

- Football pitches must have grass at 25 mm to 50 mm throughout the season, with no bare patches larger than 0.5 m² in goalmouths.
- Cricket squares must meet ECB pitch standards for the relevant club level.
- Bowling greens must be kept at a speed and trueness agreed with the clubs in a pre-season meeting.
- All arisings from sports turf must be removed. Mulched arisings are permitted only from outfield mowing.
- Parks must be litter free at the start and end of each visit.

## 2.4 Chemicals and the environment

- Any fertiliser must be slow release and applied under a nutrient plan agreed with the Council.
- Herbicide: no more than one selective application per site per year on pitches, and none on meadows, wildflower margins or within 5 metres of watercourses. PA1, PA2 and PA6 qualifications are required for application and the Council must be sent a spray record within 3 working days.
- Peat-free compost and top dressing must be used throughout.
- Biodiversity areas: 2.6 ha of Foxcote Park and 0.9 ha of Meadowgate are managed as wildlife areas, cut and collected once a year after mid-August.
- The Council is working towards a target of at least 20% battery-electric hand tools in year 1 and 50% by year 5.

## 2.5 Waste

Waste green material must be taken to a licensed composting site and weighed. Quarterly tonnage reports are required. Dog bin waste goes to the Council's transfer station. Fly-tipped waste larger than one cubic metre must be reported rather than removed.

# Section 3: Quality and Social Value Questions

Remember that the whole quality response is limited to 20 A4 pages, see section 1.2.

## Question Q1: Mobilisation and transition (weighting 6%)

Describe how you will take over the services on 1 September 2027, including the TUPE process, the transfer of knowledge about the pitches, the early renovation work that is needed in the autumn and the communication you will have with clubs and the public. (maximum 600 words)

## Question Q2: Method statement and programme (weighting 6%)

Provide a method statement and 12-month programme for pitch maintenance, including how you will plan marking, renovation and play around fixtures calendars. Explain how you will arrange resources in peak periods.

## Question Q3: Sports turf management (weighting 6%)

Explain your approach to the agronomy of the football, rugby and cricket surfaces, including how you will use soil testing, nutrient plans, aeration and overseeding to improve standards. Word count: 500

## Question Q4: Bowling greens (weighting 4%)

Explain how you will meet the standards that the bowling clubs expect, how you will schedule daily cutting, and how you will work with the clubs.

## Question Q5: Staffing, supervision and training (weighting 6%)

Describe the team to be deployed, including qualifications, supervision and holiday cover. Include details of apprenticeships and how you will maintain the Level 3 sports turf expertise.

## Question Q6: Machinery and equipment (weighting 5%)

Provide a schedule of the machinery you will use, its age and emission standard, and your plan to meet the electric tool target in section 2.4. (maximum 400 words)

## Question Q7: Quality monitoring and KPIs (weighting 5%)

Explain how you will monitor your own work, the evidence you will provide to the Council, and the key performance indicators you suggest, including a way of measuring pitch quality.

## Question Q8: Health, safety and lone working (weighting 5%)

Describe your health and safety arrangements, including risk assessments for working near the public, the safe use of heavy machinery on sports pitches and your lone-working procedure. (maximum 400 words)

## Question Q9: Play inspections and safeguarding (weighting 5%)

Explain how you will conduct play inspections to BS EN 1176 and how you will safeguard children when you work near them in parks, play areas and the leisure centre. (maximum 300 words)

## Question Q10: Environment, herbicide and biodiversity (weighting 4%)

Describe how you will reduce chemical use, protect biodiversity, use peat-free materials and reduce carbon. Word count: 350

## Question Q11: Weather resilience and business continuity (weighting 4%)

Explain how you will keep sports facilities playable in wet and dry weather and how your business would maintain service during staff absence or machinery failure.

## Question Q12: Complaints, communications and innovation (weighting 4%)

Explain how you will handle complaints from clubs and residents and give one example of how you have used innovation (for example technology, robotics or data) to improve the service on a previous contract. (maximum 300 words)

## Question Q13: Social value (weighting 10%)

Set out your measurable commitments to the Council's social value priorities: local jobs and training, support for community sports clubs and carbon reduction. Give targets, timings and how you will report. No more than 500 words, plus an optional one-page commitments table.
