# 2. Specification

## 2.1 Sites

| Site | Description | Use | Cleaning needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pellwick Village Hall | Main hall, kitchen, stage, 2 meeting rooms, toilets | Hired most days; weddings and parties on Saturdays | Clean after hire sessions; full clean weekly |
| Pellwick Memorial Hall | Hall, kitchen, 2 toilets, committee room | Hired about 4 days a week | Twice weekly plus after events |
| Hollin Green Community Centre | Hall, 3 rooms, kitchen, toilets, youth room | Used daily by groups, with a children's nursery on weekday mornings | Daily on weekdays |
| Four public toilets | Pellwick Car Park, Memorial Green, Hollin Green and the Riverside Path | Open 08:00 to 18:00 daily (to 20:00 in summer) | Daily; deep clean weekly |

## 2.2 Standards and requirements

- Work must be carried out by trained staff. BICSc training, or an equivalent, is preferred but not essential for a small contractor, if you can show an in-house training programme.
- COSHH assessments must be held for all chemicals, with safety data sheets available to the Clerk.
- Kitchens: cleaned to food hygiene standards (the halls hold a Food Hygiene Rating displayed in the kitchens). The council will not accept cleaning methods that contaminate food areas.
- Waste: take general rubbish to the bins provided; separate recycling where the council's scheme allows; any waste that is not allowed in the council's bins (for example sanitary waste from toilet units) must be removed by the Contractor under a licensed carrier.
- Hours: halls cleaned between hires and outside nursery sessions. Toilets cleaned between 07:00 and 08:00 daily and at least once more on weekends.
- The Contractor must respond to urgent issues such as vandalism, flooding or blocked toilets within 4 hours, 7 days a week.
- The nursery at Hollin Green means any person working during the day must hold a DBS check at the appropriate level. The Contractor should ask staff to complete a DBS check before starting work in the centre.

## 2.4 TUPE

The council understands that two part-time staff employed by the current provider (about 38 hours per week in total) may transfer to the new contractor. Bidders should assume TUPE applies. The council has no further employment details; it will ask the current provider to supply them to the successful tenderer.

## 2.5 Supplies

The Contractor provides cleaning chemicals and equipment. The council buys toilet rolls, soap and sanitary supplies, and the Contractor will refill dispensers.

# 4. Evaluation and Questions

## 4.0 How tenders are scored

| Criterion | Weighting |
|---|---|
| Quality (the questions below, including social value) | 60% |
| Price | 40% |

Quality answers are scored 0 to 5 (0 = no answer, 1 = poor, 2 = fair, 3 = good, 4 = very good, 5 = excellent). The score is converted to the weighting in the table by (score / 5) x weighting. The lowest price scores 40 and others score in proportion to the lowest. Social value is worth 10% inside the 60% quality total.

## 4.1 Quality questions

Questions 4.1, 4.2 and 4.3 share a combined limit of 1,000 words in total across 4.1-4.3. You may divide the words between them as you see fit.

|---|---|---|---|

# 5. Prices

## 5.1 What to give us

1. A fixed monthly price for each of the three halls.
2. A fixed monthly price for the four toilets together.
3. A price per deep clean, if different from the sums in the fixed price (please say what is included).
4. An hourly rate for extra cleaning, for example for events (normal, evening, weekend).
5. A call-out charge for emergencies outside the normal visits.
6. A one-off start-up charge, if any.

## 5.2 Price scoring

Price will be assessed as the total of 24 monthly payments for halls and toilets, plus 30 hours of extra cleaning at the normal hourly rate, plus 6 emergency call-outs at the quoted charge, plus any start-up charge. Prices are in pounds sterling excluding VAT. Fixed for the first 12 months. In the second year, price increases are limited to the increase in the National Living Wage.

## 5.3 Payment

The council pays within 30 days of a correct invoice, monthly in arrears, following approval at the council's monthly payments meeting.
