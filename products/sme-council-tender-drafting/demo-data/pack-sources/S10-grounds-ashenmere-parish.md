# Part 1: Instructions to Tenderers

## 1.1 Background

Ashenmere Parish Council (fictional) is a small rural parish council with one full-time Clerk and no direct labour. It looks after a burial ground that remains open for new burials, a closed churchyard with listed monuments, a village green, and two play areas. The Council is seeking a reliable local or regional contractor to carry out routine maintenance and to give the Clerk a simple point of contact.

## 1.2 How to apply

1. Send one email with your completed Part 3 (the Tender Response Form), your pricing form (Part 5) and your insurance certificates.
2. Attachments should be in PDF or Word format and no larger than 10 MB in total.
3. You will receive an acknowledgement within one working day. If you do not, phone the Clerk.
4. The Council will not consider tenders received after the return time.

## 1.3 General answer length rule

All answers are limited to 350 words unless a different limit is shown in the table in Part 3. Where the table shows a dash in the limit column, the general rule applies. Where the table shows a limit, that limit replaces the general rule. Anything beyond the limit will not be marked.

## 1.4 Pass/fail requirements

Tenderers must meet each of the following. Failure to do so will end the evaluation.

- Public liability insurance of at least £5 million.
- Employer's liability insurance of at least £5 million, or a written statement that you employ no staff other than yourself and are legally exempt.
- Evidence of SSIP accreditation (for example CHAS or SafeContractor), or a plan to obtain it before the contract starts.
- Turnover of at least £120,000 in the last financial year.
- PA1 and PA6 certificates (NPTC or equivalent) for anyone who will spray herbicide.
- A Modern Slavery Act declaration.
- Confirmation of compliance with UK GDPR for any personal data seen during burial and visitor work.
- Enhanced DBS checks for any staff who will inspect or work in the play areas when children are present.

## 1.5 Evaluation

The contract will be awarded to the most economically advantageous tender. The weightings are:

| Criterion | Weighting |
|---|---|
| Quality (questions Q1 to Q7) | 50% |
| Social value (question Q8) | 10% |
| Price | 40% |

Quality answers are scored from 0 to 5 using the guidance below. The score is converted into a percentage of the weighting for the question.

| Score | Description |
|---|---|
| 0 | No answer or unacceptable |
| 1 | Very poor, little relevant detail |
| 2 | Below standard, lacking detail |
| 3 | Acceptable, meets minimum |
| 4 | Good, detailed and relevant |
| 5 | Outstanding, specific, with evidence and added value |

Price will be scored out of 40, with the lowest price receiving full marks and all other prices scored in proportion to the lowest.

## 1.6 TUPE

The Council does not employ staff on these services. The current arrangement is a verbal agreement with a local sole trader who works alone. The Council does not believe TUPE applies, but tenderers should make their own assessment.

## 1.6a Selection information required

Alongside your Tender Response Form please provide, in plain English and without a separate questionnaire, the following information.

1. Your legal name, trading address, company or sole trader details and VAT status.
2. A short declaration that no director or owner has been convicted of fraud, corruption, tax evasion or modern slavery offences, and that your business is not insolvent. If anything needs to be declared, explain what you have done to put it right.
3. Your last two years of accounts or an accountant's letter, showing the turnover in section 1.4.
4. Copies of your insurance certificates and your health and safety policy.
5. A copy of your risk assessment for working in a burial ground.
6. Two references from customers with similar sites.
7. Copies of your PA1 and PA6 certificates, and the registration number of your upper-tier waste carrier licence for green waste.

The Council will check this information at the compliance stage. If any item is missing, the Clerk may give you 3 working days to supply it.

## 1.6b Performance and inspection

The contractor will be expected to attend a short walk-round with the Clerk or a Councillor every quarter. The Council will inspect each site against the standards in Part 2 and will record any defect. Defects must be put right within 7 days, or within 24 hours where they involve a risk to safety, and the contractor must send a photograph of the corrected work. Persistent failure may lead to a deduction from the monthly payment of up to 5% or, ultimately, to termination.

## 1.7 Variation and ownership

The Council may vary the area maintained by up to 10% with 4 weeks' notice, at the agreed rates. Ownership of any records of the contract, including photographs, remains with the Council.

# Part 2: Specification

## 2.1 Sites and frequencies

| Site | Area | Mowing | Other tasks | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ashenmere Burial Ground | 1.6 ha | Every 14 days from April to October, then every 28 days | Edge trimming around headstones, path weeding, hedge cut once a year in September, open-grave site tidy | Neat and respectful; grass at no more than 50 mm |
| St Wulfric's Closed Churchyard | 0.7 ha | 6 cuts a year plus a long-grass cut and collect in late August | Ivy clearing from monuments, no herbicide, monthly litter pick | Conservation grassland retained in marked areas |
| Village Green | 1.2 ha | 16 cuts a year | Bin emptying twice weekly in summer and weekly in winter, maintenance of two flower tubs | Fine turf at 30 mm for the cricket square run-off |
| Chapel Lane Play Area | 0.15 ha | 18 cuts a year | Weekly play inspection, monthly surface and fence check | In line with BS EN 1176 |
| Meadow Rise Play Area | 0.2 ha | 18 cuts a year | Weekly play inspection, monthly surface and fence check | In line with BS EN 1176 |
| Footpath edges and the Mill Pond bank | 0.4 ha | 4 strimming visits a year | Clearing of litter and fallen branches | Path width at least 1.5 m |

## 2.2 Seasonal tasks and arisings

- Spring: sweep paths, edge beds, litter pick all sites.
- Summer: water tubs weekly during dry spells, collect meadow arisings by 31 August.
- Autumn: leaf clearance from the burial ground paths and play areas at least fortnightly from October to the end of November.
- Winter: salt the Village Hall access and the burial ground car park when ice is forecast, up to 10 call-outs a year.
- All arisings are taken off site, except that mulched grass may remain on the Green when conditions allow. Wood chip may be used on the verge of the pond.

## 2.3 Herbicide, peat and biodiversity

- Herbicide may be used only by PA1/PA6 holders and only twice a year on paths in the burial ground. It must never be used in the churchyard or within 5 metres of the Mill Pond.
- Only peat-free compost may be used for planting.
- A 0.3 ha area of the Green is left as a wildflower meadow and must be cut once a year after 15 August.
- Hedges must not be cut between 1 March and 31 August without a nesting check.

## 2.4 Play inspections

The contractor will carry out and record weekly visual play inspections, report defects the same day to the Clerk, close any item that creates a serious risk and arrange an independent annual inspection by a RoSPA-registered inspector (paid for by the Council under a separate arrangement).
