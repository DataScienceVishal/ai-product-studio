# Part A: Instructions to Bidders

## A1. Summary

Hallowby Town Council (fictional) maintains a small portfolio of open spaces, two cemeteries, a market square, a community hub and three pavilions. It needs one contractor to provide grounds care, litter clearance, gritting and a handyman-style repair service, so the Council deals with one point of contact.

## A2. Response length

The whole quality response must not exceed 12 sides of A4, 11 point, excluding the Selection Questionnaire forms, certificates and policies. Individual questions below show a recommended length. Questions that show a page limit in the right-hand column must not exceed that limit. Where the column says "no limit" the question is within the overall 12 sides.

## A3. Selection and mandatory requirements

Bidders must complete the Selection Questionnaire (PPN 03/24 form, Part B) and meet all the requirements below.

- Public liability insurance of £10,000,000 and employer's liability insurance of £10,000,000.
- Turnover in the latest financial year of at least £250,000.
- CHAS or equivalent SSIP membership.
- PA1 and PA6 certificates for any herbicide operative.
- A Gas Safe, NICEIC or similar qualification only where the bidder intends to carry out gas or electrical work. Otherwise bidders must confirm they will sub-contract those tasks to registered firms.
- Modern Slavery Act compliance.
- UK GDPR compliance and a data processing agreement signed at contract award.
- Enhanced DBS for staff working at the community hub, which hosts a nursery.

## A4. Evaluation and weightings

| Criterion | Weighting |
|---|---|
| Quality (questions 1 to 10) | 40% |
| Social value (question 11) | 10% |
| Price | 50% |

Each answer is scored 0 to 5. The score is divided by 5 and multiplied by the weighting. The guide to scores is as follows.

| Score | Rating |
|---|---|
| 0 | Fails to answer |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory |
| 2 | Weak |
| 3 | Acceptable |
| 4 | Good |
| 5 | Excellent |

Price is calculated by dividing the lowest compliant price by each bid price and multiplying by 50.

## A5. TUPE

The current contractor employs 3 people on the services. The Council believes TUPE may apply to two of these. Details of age, salary and notice are in Appendix 2.

## A5a. Selection Questionnaire summary

The Selection Questionnaire (Part B form) follows the PPN 03/24 model. It is pass or fail. It covers the following.

1. Supplier details, including ownership, group structure, size and any subcontractors who will deliver more than 10% of the work.
2. Exclusion grounds. Bidders must declare any mandatory exclusion ground, such as bribery, fraud, tax offences or modern slavery offences, and any discretionary ground, such as breaches of environmental law or poor performance on a previous public contract.
3. Economic and financial standing: accounts for the last two years and confirmation of the turnover threshold.
4. Technical ability: two references from similar services in the last three years. One must be for a town or parish council or similar body.
5. Health and safety: policy, RIDDOR history, and accreditation.
6. Insurance: certificates for the required levels of cover.
7. Equality and diversity, environmental management and data protection policies.

Bidders may submit a European Single Procurement Document style form or a self-declaration if it covers the same information, and the Council will accept a signed declaration from a director.

## A5b. Contract conditions in summary

The contract is the Council's short-form services agreement. Payment is monthly in arrears within 30 days of a valid invoice. The contractor must report all accidents and incidents within 24 hours, must hold the insurance levels in A3 throughout and must allow the Council to audit its records. Either party may end the contract on 3 months' notice after the first 6 months. Service credits of up to 3% of the monthly charge apply where a KPI is missed in two consecutive months. The contractor must not subcontract more than 20% of the contract value without consent.

## A5c. Quality inspections and complaints during the contract

The Council will inspect sites at least fortnightly during the growing season and monthly in winter. The Deputy Town Clerk will log defects and the contractor must acknowledge each in one working day and rectify within the response times in Appendix 1. Complaints from residents will be passed to the contractor, who must reply to the Council within 2 working days.

## A6. Clarifications

All questions to the Deputy Town Clerk through the portal. The answers will be anonymised and shared.

# Part B: Specification

## B1. Site schedule

| Site | Area | Mowing | Seasonal and other tasks | Standards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Market Square and Gardens | 0.6 ha | 20 cuts, 4 bedding changes | Planters (36), pressure washing twice a year, daily litter pick | Presentable by 08:00 on market days |
| Hallowby Cemetery | 2.4 ha | 12 cuts | Paths weeded three times a year, hedge cut in September, memorial safety check once a year | Grass no more than 75 mm |
| Old Cemetery and Chapel Garden | 1.1 ha | 8 cuts plus long-grass area | Ivy clearing, no herbicide | Wildlife areas respected |
| Hub Gardens | 0.5 ha | 18 cuts | Raised vegetable beds support, litter pick, weekly checks of the nursery fence | DBS staff only |
| Three pavilion sites | 3.0 ha in total | 16 cuts | Line-marking of one pitch, goalpost checks | Safe for play |
| Canal Towpath verges | 1.8 km | 6 cuts | Strimming, litter, vegetation clearance | Path 2 m wide |

## B2. Litter and bins

- Daily litter pick in the Market Square and Hub Gardens, 5 days a week.
- Weekly litter pick elsewhere.
- Empty and clean 62 bins, with a minimum frequency of twice a week in summer.

## B3. Winter gritting

Pre-treat the Market Square, Hub car park and cemetery entrances on forecast days from 1 November to 31 March, with response within 2 hours of instruction. Up to 20 call-outs a winter.

## B4. Small facilities tasks

Minor repairs such as benches, gates, fence panels, signage, graffiti removal, moss removal and emergency boarding, up to a value of £250 per job without prior approval. Call-out response in 4 hours for urgent safety work and 5 working days for routine jobs.

## B5. Herbicide and the environment

- Herbicide to be used on hard surfaces only, no more than twice a year, by PA1 and PA6 holders, and never within 5 metres of the canal.
- Only peat-free compost.
- Use of battery-powered tools where practicable.
- Wildlife areas include the Old Cemetery long-grass area and the towpath verges, cut and collect once after mid-August.

## B6. Waste

Green waste to a licensed facility with waste transfer notes. Fly-tipping up to one cubic metre to be removed. Records provided quarterly.
