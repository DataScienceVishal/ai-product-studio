## Lessons for the validator and next scout
- Dynamic pricing: every consumer-facing niche has a platform feature (round 14), and the SMB niches left (forecourts, e-commerce, scrap, boutique markdowns, menus) either have a cheap or free vendor or lack any lawful data to prove uplift. Fuel Finder is the first lawful public price feed found, and it still lost, because the incumbents (EdgePetrol, myAutomate) were already built on it and sales volume is the missing variable. This is the fifth pricing idea killed on the data red flag or on a platform feature. Stop the thesis until a data partner exists.
- The "messy ledger" pattern keeps hitting the same wall: the job is either one-off (clean-up, capital allowances), deterministic (commission, unbilled time), or already sold inside Dext, Xero or FreeAgent (VAT checks). The holiday-pay look-back remains the only idea where no incumbent documents the job.
- Pay evidence is again the hole: only listing prices of adjacent tools, never a price for the judgement job itself.
- Recommendation (agrees with round 18): further desk rounds on Vishal's edges are unlikely to help. Buyer-side evidence (Q-9, BUYER-INTERVIEW.md) is the better use of time. If one more desk check is wanted, run it on capital-versus-revenue classification only after a buyer says it costs hours.
