## Lessons
- Booking and PMS platforms now ship rule-based or AI dynamic pricing in almost every consumer-facing small-operator niche (storage, courts, camping, salons, boats, coworking, golf). The pricing job is a platform feature, so it fails Competition before Pay.
- The one niche without an SMB tool (hire) is B2B, quote-led, and the data sits in the hire system vendor's hands. Vendors there (Point of Rental, MCS) are already advertising AI Insights.
- Uplift cannot be proven without the operator's real history. Synthetic simulation proves the method only, so the data red flag bites every pricing idea.
- Vendor "+30% revenue" claims (Book and Go, Padel Passport) have no stated method; independent uplift data was not found anywhere.
- The thesis should stop unless there is a way to get a real operator export; that needs Vishal's contact route (selling is locked).
