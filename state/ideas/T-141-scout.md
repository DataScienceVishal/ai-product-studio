# T-141 scout: jobs SMEs already outsource at a listed price (2026-11-26)

Method: started from outsourced jobs with a public hourly, per-item or monthly price, then checked incumbents. 17 WebSearch calls, 0 fetches. All evidence below is WebSearch result summaries dated 2026-11-26. No vendor page was fetched in full, so every incumbent claim is "search evidence" and the wedge claims are unverified. No injection attempts seen. Nobody contacted. No other file touched. Did not read round summaries beyond round 20, RANKING.md and the killed names; none of the 8 candidates below repeats a killed or ranked idea (closest: AP/statement work vs the killed sage-remittance-cash-allocator; deductions vs the parked fmcg-distributor-claim-reconciler, which is India and scheme-circular based).

Result: 8 candidates tested, 0 KEEP, 8 DROP. Nearest to viable: candidate 8 (retailer deductions), which fails only the price-evidence bar.

## Candidates

### 1. Supplier statement reconciliation (AP clerk or offshore AP firm)
- Pitch: AI matches each supplier's monthly statement to the ledger and lists missing bills, duplicates and credit notes.
- Buyer: SMEs with a purchase ledger in Xero or QuickBooks.
- Pain: Xero Ideas forum has a request thread "Bills - AI reconciliation of supplier statements" (https://productideas.xero.com/forums/939198-for-small-businesses/suggestions/47511881-bills-ai-reconciliation-of-supplier-statements); Spendesk and Corpay publish month-end how-tos on it (https://www.spendesk.com/blog/supplier-statement-reconciliation-month-end/, https://www.corpay.com/es-419/resources/blog/vendor-statement-reconciliation).
- Price: APMatching from GBP 560 a month (https://www.softwareadvice.com/za/accounting/automated-supplier-statement-reconciliation-profile/); AP Accurate indicative US$300-5,000 a month (https://enterprisedna.co/omni/instead-of/ap-accurate/pricing). Both are software, no managed-service price found.
- Incumbent: Dext sells Supplier Statements reconciliation against Xero and QuickBooks Online on all plans (https://dext.com/en/business/products/supplier-statements-reconciliation); Statement-Matching.com for SAP.
- Verdict: DROP. Dext already does it inside the tool the bookkeeper uses.

### 2. Check-out versus check-in inventory comparison (independent inventory clerks)
- Pitch: AI compares check-in and check-out reports and photos and drafts the deposit-deduction schedule.
- Buyer: letting agents and landlords.
- Pain: Renters' Rights Act phase 1 makes inventories matter for deposit disputes (https://letsafeuk.co.uk/landlord-property-inventory-uk); guide notes the two reports must be comparable (https://www.mydeposits.co.uk/content-hub/inventories-the-complete-guide/).
- Price: check-out report GBP 80-120 for a 2-bed (https://letsafeuk.co.uk/landlord-property-inventory-uk, sales page); Prospect lists 4-bed GBP 186 and 5-bed GBP 216 (https://www.prospect.co.uk/check-out-reports); Augusta says clerks charge GBP 80-200 (https://www.augustapp.com/blog/best-inventory-services-for-landlords).
- Incumbent: Property Inspect (Inspect AI, built in), PropCheck AI (AI Compare ranks rooms by change), KapturAI (auto-compares and flags dilapidations), Inventory Base (BaseAI), Landlords Guild Inventory Compare tool (250 credits per report), all per the search summary (https://propertyinspect.com/move-in-move-out-inspections/, https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1108039/PropCheckAI, https://england.landlordsguild.com/article/new-ai-inventory-compare-tool-for-landlords).
- Verdict: DROP. Five products ship the comparison; wedge none.

### 3. Quantity takeoff for subcontractors (outsourced estimators)
- Pitch: AI takeoff from drawings for UK trade subcontractors.
- Buyer: small UK trades quoting from PDFs.
- Pain: not separately evidenced; only listings.
- Price: takeoff-only GBP 60-160 per A1/A3 drawing, residential estimate packs GBP 180-480 (https://costestimator.co.uk/maximising-your-roi-how-estimator-services-can-help, vendor benchmarks); Fiverr UK estimators (e.g. https://fiverr.com/engr_yawar/do-uk-building-estimate).
- Incumbent: TradeOff (UK subcontractors, GBP 29 and GBP 79 a month with AI quantity extraction; currency unverified, https://www.getapp.com/all-software/a/tradeoff/), Kreo from about GBP 95 (https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/1014073/kreo-2d-takeoff), Togal.AI USD 299 a month (https://togal.ai/pricing).
- Verdict: DROP. Priced at GBP 29-95 a month by several AI vendors.

### 4. Parcel-carrier invoice audit and refund claims (agencies on contingency)
- Pitch: AI audits courier invoices for overcharges and late-delivery refunds.
- Buyer: UK ecommerce merchants.
- Pain: vendors cite 2-8% of carrier invoice value as refund-eligible (vendor claim, https://www.threecolts.com/blog/what-is-parcel-auditing/); ShippyPro notes UK carriers each use their own column layout (https://www.shippypro.com/blog/en/shipping-financial-intelligence-agentic-carrier-invoice-audit).
- Price: providers take 25-50% of recovered amounts or a plan fee (https://www.sendcloud.com/en-uk/blog/best-parcel-audit-software-for-ecommerce/, summary only).
- Incumbent: Claimit launched an AI courier invoice auditing tool for UK retailers in September 2026 (https://channelx.world/2026/09/clamit-launches-ai-courier-invoice-auditing-tool-to-rid-retailers-of-mystery-overcharges/); LateShipment agentic audit (https://www.lateshipment.com/features/audit-refunds/); ShippyPro; nShift; Sendcloud lists 9 tools.
- Verdict: DROP. Nine-plus tools including a UK one launched two months ago.

### 5. IR35 status determination (outsourced assessors, per contract)
- Pitch: AI reads a contract and working practices and drafts the status determination for agencies and end clients.
- Buyer: recruitment agencies and mid-size end clients.
- Pain: agencies and clients carry the liability under the off-payroll rules; Qdos sells a status review for them (https://www.goqdos.com/ir35/off-payroll-working/status-review).
- Price: Qdos full contract review GBP 125 + VAT (https://qdoscontractor.com/full-ir35-contract-review); IPSE GBP 192 inc VAT (https://www.ipse.co.uk/contract-review); Kingsbridge GBP 50 + VAT (https://www.kingsbridge.co.uk/products/ir35-status-review/).
- Incumbent: Crunch credits from GBP 10 per assessment (https://ir35.crunch.co.uk/employers/pricing); CoComply digital assessments at GBP 149 with AI/ML (G-Cloud pricing document in the search results); Qdos Status Review auto-issues the SDS.
- Verdict: DROP. The per-item price is already GBP 10 and falling, and several firms ship it.

### 6. Product data entry and enrichment for ecommerce (offshore per-SKU)
- Pitch: AI turns supplier spreadsheets and PDFs into publish-ready product records.
- Buyer: small online retailers and distributors.
- Pain: offshore keying is 30-60 records an hour, enrichment 2-6 SKUs an hour (https://www.anglera.com/guides/offshore-data-entry-vs-automated-enrichment, vendor guide).
- Price: USD 0.30-2.50 per SKU keying and USD 3-12+ per SKU enrichment (same Anglera guide, single vendor); Outsource2India from USD 6 an hour (https://www.outsource2india.com/DataManagement/sku-data-entry-services.asp); Clutch listing USD 156 a month for 10 hours a week (https://clutch.co/go-to-review/ask-data-entry/278071).
- Incumbent: Anglera sells automated enrichment (the source of the guide). Already near the killed merchant-supplier-price-file-loader (TableFlow, Data Yard).
- Verdict: DROP. The price source is an AI competitor and the space is closed.

### 7. Block management service-charge accounts and invoice coding (managing agents)
- Pitch: AI codes invoices to service-charge heads per block and drafts the year-end reconciled accounts pack.
- Buyer: small UK managing agents and RTM companies.
- Pain: a trade article calls invoice-to-ledger the main bottleneck (https://www.flat-living.co.uk/?p=25800); TPI advice note on fees (https://www.tpi.org.uk/media/tr3d5gvz/tpi-advice-note-management-fees.pdf).
- Price: Ringley GBP 875 + VAT a year for 4-8 units plus GBP 75 per extra unit (https://ringley.co.uk/block_management/service_levels); TPI worked example GBP 6,000 for 30 units. These are whole-service management fees, not the accounts job, so Pay is unproven.
- Incumbent: MRI block management has service-charge accounting controls and AI only for lease abstraction (blog, https://sagnikbhattacharya.com/blog/block-management-software-service-charge-repairs); Fixflo; Alto help centre has a block and service charge article (https://support.altosoftware.co.uk/hc/en-gb/articles/5051620077087). No AI service-charge product found, but only blog evidence and the core is deterministic coding.
- Verdict: DROP. No listed price for the job, thin pain evidence, mostly deterministic.

### 8. Retailer deduction and chargeback disputes for UK food suppliers (contingency recovery firms)
- Pitch: AI reads a retailer remittance, validates each deduction against orders, delivery data and agreed terms, and drafts the dispute inside the time limit.
- Buyer: UK food and drink producers selling to the 14 designated grocers, plus Amazon Vendor suppliers.
- Pain: the Groceries Code Adjudicator's 2026 supplier survey lists not allowing 30 days to challenge proposed invoice deductions, and deducting even if challenged, as recurring issues (YouGov deep-dive, https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6ab54e4a8cb3c0580e5011b2/2026_GCA_conference_slide_deck.pdf, https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/68dd3fdb8c1db6022d0c9fbb/YouGov_slides_-_GCA_Annual_conference_2025.pdf); the GCA annual report 2025-26 says it is looking at deduction handling (https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6a588b4a31fb6daf314137a4/Groceries_Code_Adjudicator_Annual_Report_and_Accounts_2025-2026.pdf); SPS Commerce and Amazon Vendor guides describe deductions as a leading margin leak (https://www.spscommerce.com/community/articles/understanding-retailer-deductions-chargebacks-and-fines, https://merchantspring.io/resources/stop-profit-leaks-how-to-tackle-amazon-vendor-chargebacks).
- Price: only contingency benchmarks, none for this job: Liberata duplicate-payment recovery 25% standard, 12.5% minimum (G-Cloud pricing doc, https://assets.applytosupply.digitalmarketplace.service.gov.uk/g-cloud-15/documents/92302/532464089949551-pricing-document-2026-01-30-0718.pdf); third-party deduction services take a contingency fee (https://racklify.com/encyclopedia/how-to-build-a-deduction-recovery-workflow-for-retail-suppliers/, vendor). That is one real price point and one unquantified, so the 2-price bar is not met.
- Incumbent: SPS Commerce Revenue Recovery and Esker (food and beverage, UK-language pages) and HighRadius sell deduction management; Celonis with Valence uses AI to gather evidence (https://itwire.com/business-it-news/business-intelligence/celonis-and-valence-use-ai-to-slash-invoice-deduction-disputes-from-weeks-to-days). ArpFlow serves emerging CPG brands with outcome-based pricing (https://www.g2.com/products/arpflow/discuss, unverified). All are US-led or enterprise; UK retailer portal coverage unchecked.
- Wedge (unproven): small UK suppliers priced out of enterprise tools; a GCA forcing function (investigation into deductions and the 30-day challenge window). Risks: retailer remittance data is private (demo only on synthetic remittances), the AI step is mostly matching, ArpFlow may serve the same segment.
- Verdict: DROP for now, because Pay is unproven and a retailer-portal data red flag applies. If anyone retries: fetch the SPS, Esker and ArpFlow pricing and UK coverage pages, read the GCA annual report text on deductions, and find a UK deduction-recovery firm's published fee.

## Lessons for the next scout
- Per-item outsourced prices exist for every job here, but AI vendors had already collapsed them: IR35 GBP 10 to 149, takeoff GBP 29 to 95 a month, supplier statements inside Dext. Where an outsourced price is listed publicly, an AI vendor has usually listed one too. Check the "AI price" first.
- Offshore price guides are often written by AI competitors (Anglera). Treat them as vendor claims.
- The only open area in this sample is the one with no public price (retailer deductions, block accounts): the same Pay hole as rounds 6, 10 and 20. Desk research alone cannot fix it; a buyer or recovery-firm quote is needed.
- Agrees with rounds 18-20: desk rounds on SME document or ledger work return mostly kills.
