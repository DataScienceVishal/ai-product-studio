## Lessons
1. In switchover jobs the platform owner pays for the data move. Xero (Movemybooks) and QuickBooks give the conversion away, and BrightPay imports YTD for free. So Pay is zero for the "move" and the only priced work is the human clean-up afterwards (Osome £850 a year, Fiverr $200-$400).
2. No UK firm publishes a fixed fee for a migration or a per-year catch-up. Search only returned vendor subscription tiers and offshore marketplace gigs. The 2-price-point bar is hard to meet for this whole angle.
3. MMC Convert (a human-run service with 90% five-star reviews, and exclusive partner status with several platforms) already covers the data types an AI mapper would handle, and sells chart-of-accounts mapping as an add-on.
4. The best remaining shape is the accountant's unbilled catch-up time, which needs a practice-owner source quoting hours per backlog before it can be scored. Check Sleek's AI claim on its own pages and open the two free backlog tools first (fetches on the Sleek bookkeeping page and Xero change-to-xero page failed in this run).
