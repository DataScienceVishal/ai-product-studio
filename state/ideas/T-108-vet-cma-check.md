**Verdict: KILL-candidate (park only if the validator wants a second look). Would-be score 54 to 56, no path to 70.**

## Answer to "does anyone charge vets for CMA monitoring?"
Yes, but cheaply and without software monitoring.
- Xonvet (https://xonvet-website.pages.dev/, accessed 2026-11-26) sells UK independent practices "Compliant" at £189 one-off plus £19/month (price widget for the 36 services) and "Confident" at £449 one-off plus £19/month (estimate template, complaint log, audit checklists, own-brand and cremation disclosures). Free 30-minute consult. No named operator on the page. The "monitoring" is checklists and a responsibility matrix, not software reading invoices.
- Pricebook.vet (https://pricebook.vet/) is "free forever", £0/month; no complaints log, estimate or monitoring shown on the page.
- Lupa (https://lupapets.com/blog/cma-pricing-transparency-compliance-guide/, 9 Jul 2026) builds itemised invoices, estimates and the price list source into the PMS; no complaints log or £500 alert mentioned; no price on the page.
- Provet (https://www.provet.com/uk-cma-reforms): still no complaints module and none planned; no £500 threshold alert (it flags a consultation passing its own estimate total); prescription fee automation and own-brand flagging in development; bundle export needs a CSM. Core £139 per vet/month, Pro £159 per vet plus £199 platform (https://provet.com/pricing); no CMA add-on priced.
- Not found on own pages: ezyVet, Covetrus (RxWorks, RoboVet), Merlin, VetIT. Searches returned nothing, so that is absence of evidence, not proof of a gap.
