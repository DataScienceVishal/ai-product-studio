## Top candidate and bundle effect

No strong KEEP, so no new G0 idea file was written. The best of the set is **#1 AE reconciliation**, and only as a WATCH: it has the largest official enforcement volume, but the volume is not tied to contribution maths and free provider and in-run checks surround it. If the validator wants a bundle test, the cheapest module path is #3 (leaver holiday), which reuses the ingest in full but adds no new buyer pain. None of the three would raise rank-1's Pay score: Pay needs a published software price for a look-back, and no candidate produced one. The only new Pay-adjacent points are PayslipIQ's £19 one-off per payslip (individuals, not on sale) and Employment Hero's £400 payroll database audit fee (search summary, https://employmenthero.com/uk/legals/service-terms-and-conditions/scope-of-services-uk/), which is a data-transfer fee, not a compliance review.

Effect on rank 1: add PayslipIQ Business to the kill test. Its Business page names "holiday-pay miscalculations" but it is a waitlist with no date, no price and no mention of a 52-week or look-back method. If it ships a holiday-pay look-back, Competition falls to 2 under the existing kill rule.

## Lessons

1. Buyer-first found no recurring paid job over the same data that is not already inside payroll software or a free regulator or provider check. The three jobs where the engine would add value (AE, SSP, leaver holiday) are all calculated in-run by the payroll package, so what remains is a one-off clean-up, not a subscription.
2. The new competitive risk is a generic "drop your payroll CSV, get anomalies" tool (PayslipIQ). It is cheap and planned but not built. A narrow, deep, rule-exact module (holiday pay look-back with working shown) is the opposite of that, which supports keeping rank 1 narrow rather than widening to a family.
3. TPR publishes action counts but not causes. Without causes, enforcement volume is not proof that a reconciliation tool has a market. Check the TPR compliance report cause tables before scoring any AE idea.
4. No published price exists for look-back or reconciliation software in UK payroll; the only public prices are hourly bureau extras and one-off human reviews. Pay stays the binding constraint across the family.
5. Not done in this time box: BrightPay, Staffology, Xero help pages for SSP and AE look-back reports; People's Pension, Nest checker pages (403 or not reached); primary ERA 2025 commencement dates; SSP penalty rules on gov.uk. A follow-up could close these before any WATCH is promoted.
