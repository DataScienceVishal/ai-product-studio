**Pattern so far:** every idea lost most of its points on Competition (2 to 5 out of 10). By late 2026 incumbents ship "AI over your documents" as a standard or free feature, so a gap has to be checked on the competitor's own product pages, and the stronger wedges are ones incumbents can't easily follow: a regulatory deadline, deep domain workflow, a channel they don't serve, or liability they won't take.
