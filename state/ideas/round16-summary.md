## Lessons
- The 2027 calendar is thinner than it looks. Three headline items (Companies House filing, UK REACH, Commercial Payments Bill) moved or are not dated, and two (Ofcom, product safety) have no SME duty starting in 2027. Check the current gov.uk page before building a thesis on a "2027" date.
- Where a rule does start in 2027 and is document-heavy (PRS database, Martyn's Law), the nearest vendor or the regulator itself had already shipped the tool or a free template by November 2026, within weeks of the guidance. Forcing functions this visible are served first.
- Martyn's Law standard tier is the largest reach found (about 155,000 premises) but has no submitted document, a free regulator tool and free training. Pay is the gap: no priced substitute was found.
- Recommendation: a 2027-rules thesis is exhausted for AI document jobs. If a later round reuses it, wait for a made SI with a commencement date and a published fee or form (for example the Martyn's Law commencement regulations or the PRS database evidence schema) before searching vendors.
