# Comprehension brief template (teacher-style)

Audience: Vishal, who must demo the product and answer a buyer's questions. He learns best with a picture first, then a small worked example, then depth. Plain English; define each term the first time.

## 1. One-picture overview
A diagram of user → app → AI → data, with every arrow labelled by what flows along it.

## 2. The 60-second story
The problem, who has it, what the product does, and why AI is essential to it.

## 3. One small example, end to end
A tiny realistic case (for example "a café with 6 staff asks for next week's rota"), showing each step's input and output.

## 4. How each part works
For each component: what it does, the key code paths (file paths), the decisions and trade-offs (link the ADRs), its failure modes and what the user sees when it fails.

## 5. Buyer Q&A
Fifteen likely buyer questions with honest answers: price, data security, accuracy, what happens when the AI is wrong, setup time, integrations, UK GDPR and DPDP, support.

## 6. Demo map
Each step of the demo script mapped to the part of the system it shows.

## 7. Limits and roadmap
What it can't do yet, and what's next.

## 8. Self-check quiz
Ten questions, answers at the bottom.
